❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Outfique, including information about subscriptions, cancellations, and refunds.

Can I get my money back after cancelling my subscription?

Yes! We offer a full refund if you cancel your subscription within 14 days of your initial purchase. After this period, refunds are processed on a pro-rata basis for any unused portion of your subscription period.

Refund Policy:
  • Full refund within 14 days of purchase
  • Pro-rata refund after 14 days based on unused time
  • Refunds processed within 5-7 business days
  • Original payment method will be credited
How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. Navigate to Account → Subscription → Cancel Subscription. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.

What happens to my saved outfits when I cancel?

All your saved outfits and uploaded clothing items remain accessible for 30 days after cancellation. You can re-subscribe at any time to regain full access. After 30 days, your data will be archived, but you can request restoration within 90 days.

Are there any cancellation fees?

No, there are absolutely no cancellation fees. You can cancel anytime without any penalties or hidden charges.

Is Outfique really free to use?

Yes! Outfique offers a free tier that includes basic outfit creation and management features. Premium subscriptions unlock advanced AI-powered outfit generation, unlimited storage, and exclusive style recommendations.

How do I request a refund?

To request a refund, please contact our support team at support@outfique.com with your account details and reason for the refund. We aim to process all refund requests within 2 business days.

Can I pause my subscription instead of cancelling?

Yes! You can pause your subscription for up to 3 months without losing your data or preferences. This is a great option if you need a temporary break but plan to return to Outfique.

Need More Help?

If you have additional questions or concerns that aren't covered here, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team at support@outfique.com. We're here to help!
