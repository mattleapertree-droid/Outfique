💡 FAQ & Help Center

Subscription & Billing

Q: Can I get my money back after cancelling my subscription?

Yes! Outfique offers a flexible refund policy for subscription cancellations:

  • Within 7 days: Full refund, no questions asked
  • Within 30 days: Prorated refund based on unused service time
  • After 30 days: No refund, but you retain access until the end of your billing period
Important: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after cancellation request approval. The amount will be credited to your original payment method.
Q: How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancelling your Outfique subscription is easy:

  1. Log in to your account
  2. Go to "Account Settings"
  3. Click on "Subscription Management"
  4. Select "Cancel Subscription"
  5. Follow the prompts to confirm your cancellation

You will receive a confirmation email once your cancellation is processed.

Q: What happens to my saved outfits after cancellation?

Your saved outfits and wardrobe data remain accessible in read-only mode for 90 days after cancellation. During this time, you can:

  • View and download your saved outfits
  • Export your wardrobe data
  • Reactivate your subscription without losing any data

After 90 days, data may be permanently deleted unless you reactivate your subscription.

Q: Are there any cancellation fees?

No cancellation fees! Outfique believes in transparency and flexibility. You can cancel anytime without any hidden charges or penalties.

General Questions

Q: What is Outfique?

Outfique is a smart fashion website system that helps you create outfit combinations using the clothes you already own. Upload images of your clothing and generate creative outfit ideas with our AI-powered tools.

Q: Is Outfique free to use?

Outfique offers both free and premium subscription plans:

  • Free Plan: Basic outfit creation with limited AI generations (5 per month)
  • Premium Plan: Unlimited AI generations, advanced styling tools, and exclusive collections
Q: How does the AI outfit generator work?

Our AI uses advanced image generation technology (powered by Stable Diffusion) to create outfit combinations based on:

  • Your uploaded clothing items
  • Style preferences you specify
  • Current fashion trends
  • Seasonal recommendations

Technical Support

Q: What browsers are supported?

Outfique works best on modern browsers including:

  • Google Chrome (recommended)
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari (macOS/iOS)
  • Microsoft Edge
Q: What image formats are supported for uploads?

You can upload images in the following formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG. For best results, use high-quality images with good lighting and clear views of the clothing items.
